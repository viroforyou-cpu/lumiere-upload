# 02-month — Monthly dashboard (owner / administrator)

Professional, modern, dark background with gold accent, bilingual (EN/ES). Generated by the
analytics engine from `analytics/output/` for the calendar month. Rebuilt from records —
never hand-edited for numbers.

## Views shown

`monthly_revenue`, `gender_segments`, `retention_rate`, `returning_after_1_2_months`,
`seasonality`, `commission_totals`, `top_clients`

Labels are rendered from `_shared/i18n/en.md` and `es.md` — the dashboard stores only keys,
never the English/Spanish text itself.

## Rules

- Retention and returning rates come from `analytics/output/returning-after-1-2-months.md`.
- Commission totals mirror `settlement/` output; receptionists have no access here.
