# 01-week — Weekly dashboard (owner / administrator)

Professional, modern, dark background with gold accent, bilingual (EN/ES). Generated by the
analytics engine from `analytics/output/` for the operating week (Mon–Sat). Rebuilt from
records — never hand-edited for numbers.

## Views shown

`weekly_revenue`, `service_mix`, `staff_performance`, `new_clients`, `returning_clients`,
`stock_movement`, `top_clients`

Labels are rendered from `_shared/i18n/en.md` and `es.md` — the dashboard stores only keys,
never the English/Spanish text itself.

## Notes

- Staff performance includes new/returning clients per stylist but **not** commission amounts;
  commission totals live on `dashboards/02-month.md` and `settlement/`.
- Receptionists have no access to this dashboard.
