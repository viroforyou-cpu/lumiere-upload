# Service Menu

Services are grouped by discipline and by gender line. Prices live in `pricing.md` (ARS).
Services have no duration fields — there is no timing for services.

## Hair

| Group | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Cut | Men's cut, clipper cut, fade | Women's cut, layered cut, trim |
| Color | Men's color, touch-up | Full color, highlights, balayage, gloss |
| Treatments | Scalp treatment, keratin | Keratin, deep conditioning, olaplex |

## Nails

| Group | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Manicure | Men's manicure, clean-up | Classic manicure, gel, acrylic, dip |
| Pedicure | Men's pedicure, clean-up | Classic pedicure, gel, spa pedicure |

## Beauty

| Group | Men | Women |
|---|---|---|
| Face | Men's facial, waxing (beard) | Facial, waxing, lash lift, lash extensions |
| Brows | Brows grooming | Brow shaping, brow tint, lamination |

## Staff assignment

- A **stylist** performs the service (cut, color, style, nails, beauty).
- An **assistant** mainly washes clients' hair and supports the stylist during the service.
- Each service may have both a stylist and an assistant assigned — both are recorded on the
  ticket for commission attribution.
