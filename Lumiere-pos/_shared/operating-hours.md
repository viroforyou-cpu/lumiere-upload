## Rules

- The operating week runs **Monday to Saturday**. Weekly settlement happens at
  **Saturday close**.
- Services have **no timing/duration** — clients are not billed by minutes. Appointments are
  calendar slots only.
- Receptionists: one works the **morning shift**, one the **afternoon shift** (see `roles.md`).
